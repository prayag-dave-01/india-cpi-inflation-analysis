# Key Insights

## 1. Broader Category Contribution
Food represents 44% of the classified observations in the workbook's current contribution analysis. Education represents approximately 3%.

## 2. YoY CPI Inflation
The Rural + Urban CPI series records its highest year-on-year increase from 2017 onward in 2022 at 6.6%.

## 3. Food MoM Inflation
February 2023 records the highest observed monthly movement in the workbook's selected food analysis at approximately 7.1%, primarily driven by Fruits. December 2022 records the lowest at approximately -12.7%, primarily driven by Vegetables.

## 4. COVID-19 Period
The workbook compares Food and Beverages, Health, and Household Goods and Services before and after the 2020 period. The selected-category average is shown as approximately 5.2% before 2020 and 5.87% after 2020.

## 5. Fuel & Transport
Fuel and Transport & Communication show a positive correlation of approximately 67% in the workbook's 2021–2023 month-on-month analysis. Fuel and Education show approximately 34% positive correlation.

## Interpretation Note
Correlation measures association, not causation. The category-contribution chart uses the workbook's current count-based calculation and should not be interpreted as an official CPI expenditure-weight calculation.
